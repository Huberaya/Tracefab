TRACEFAB — the intelligence layer for the global textile supply chain

One intelligence layer. Eight working surfaces.

TRACEFAB is not a QR-code generator and not a single dashboard. It is the structured data layer where suppliers, products, materials, evidence and quality connect — and from which every Digital Product Passport is only a projection.

01 — The eight pillars
01

Supply chain

Map the complete chain — from fiber to finished product — tier by tier, site by site.

02

Product intelligence

Understand each product at material, component, supplier, factory and process level.

03

Data collection

Request structured information from suppliers, with templates, deadlines and versioned answers.

04

Evidence

Documents, certificates and proofs — private by default, hashed, linked and reviewed.

05

Quality

Measure completeness, evidence coverage, verification coverage, freshness and consistency. Every score is explainable.

06

Traceability

Connect data to each other: materials, lots, facilities, documents — into one auditable lineage.

07

Intelligence

Spot risks, anomalies and gaps on your own data — answers always cite their sources.

08

DPP & Passport

Turn structured, evidenced data into DPP readiness and public product experiences.

02 — How it works
01

Connect

Invite suppliers and teams into a shared, tenant-isolated workspace.

02

Collect

Send structured data requests with deadlines and automatic reminders.

03

Evidence

Attach documents and certifications; nothing is verified silently.

04

Verify

Review provenance and quality; declarations never become proofs on their own.

05

Trace

Everything connects into product intelligence, ready for DPP.
