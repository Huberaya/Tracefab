The Supply Chain Reality

End the chaos of scattered PDFs, emails, and unverified declarations.

Textile supply chains are notoriously fragmented across tiers. Without a structured data platform, compliance is impossible to prove.

The Fragmented Workflow

How brands struggle today across siloed teams and legacy tools.

  • Endless Excel spreadsheets: Outdated supplier files with broken versioning.
  • Unindexed PDF certificates: Expirations missed; no connection to SKUs.
  • Blind declarations: Unverified claims that expose brands to greenwashing fines.
  • DPP gridlock: Missing batch records prevent digital passport issuance.

The TRACEFAB Infrastructure

Automated, verified, structured and connected directly to production.

  • Structured schema: Normalized textile data models for fibers, weaves, yarns, and finishes.
  • Evidence-backed repository: Every transaction certificate is cryptographically linked to SKUs.
  • Granular Data Quality score: Understand exactly what is Declared, Documented, or Certified.
  • One-click DPP generation: Compliant JSON-LD data exports ready for consumer QR codes.

Explainable Data Quality Scale

No arbitrary 5-star ratings. Tracefab evaluates supplier statements across explicit, legally defensible tiers of trust.

Complete Level 6

100% Fully Verified

All required datapoints are populated, cross-audited, and certified with active chain of custody.

Verified Level 5

Reviewer Verified

Internal compliance or brand reviewer has inspected and validated all submitted supporting documents.

Certified Level 4

Third-Party Audited

GOTS, GRS, Oeko-Tex, or Bluesign audit certificate attached and matched to specific batch lot.

Documented Level 3

Evidence Attached

Supplier has uploaded a lab test, bill of lading, or declaration file awaiting human validation.

Declared Level 2

Self-Stated Claim

Supplier stated data in a questionnaire without accompanying cryptographic proof or lab document.

Needs Review Action Needed

Discrepancy or Missing

Required mandatory field is incomplete, expired certificate detected, or data flagged for revision.
