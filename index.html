Certified

Third-Party Audited Fact

Backed by an accredited transaction certificate (GOTS, GRS, Oeko-Tex) verified against batch delivery notes.

Verified

Reviewer Verified Evidence

Supporting lab test or shipping bill inspected and approved by a qualified brand reviewer or audit authority.

Documented

Evidence Attached

File or lab document uploaded by supplier and awaiting automated OCR and human inspection.

Declared

Self-Stated Claim

Entered in a questionnaire form without accompanying cryptographic third-party certification.

Needs Review

Discrepancy Detected

Missing
