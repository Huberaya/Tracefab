01 — The connected foundation

Your entire textile ecosystem. One connected data foundation.

A textile chain is not a list of suppliers. It is a sequence of transformations, each with its own company, site, certificate, document and level of proof. TRACEFAB holds all of it in one continuous model — and lets you read it one dimension at a time, or all at once.
